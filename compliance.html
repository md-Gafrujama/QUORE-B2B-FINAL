Governance in practice

What happens to a record before it reaches you

Compliance isn't a policy page for us - it's steps in the delivery pipeline every lead moves through.

01

Sourced from owned data

The record originates in our proprietary database, activated under CAN-SPAM - never a rented or resold third-party file we can't vouch for.

02

Matched & suppressed

Contacts are matched to your ICP, filters, and target accounts. Your suppression lists are applied and records are deduplicated before anyone is contacted.

03

Human tele-verification

A live agent confirms identity, interest, and - where the program calls for it - BANT and your custom qualifying questions. Records that don't clear are dropped.

04

Opt-out honored

Unsubscribe and do-not-contact requests are honored and suppressed from future outreach, in line with CAN-SPAM.

05

Delivered with a trail

Clean, format-matched records are delivered to your field map, each traceable to its source, qualification, and verification. Anything out of spec is replaced.
