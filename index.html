How it works

From order to delivery - a fixed pipeline, not guesswork

Every program runs through the same controlled flow: your spec defines the inputs, our proprietary database and verification stack process each record, and only leads that pass the quality gate ship to you.

Input · S(spec)

Define the order

You set asset, geo, titles, volume, custom qualifiers, and the exact field map. We confirm feasibility and a per-lead rate before activation.

Source · D → A → E

Source, activate & engage

We pull from our proprietary B2B database, activate outreach under CAN-SPAM, and engage contacts against your content or qualification script.

Filter · f(ICP)

Profile & match

Firmographic, title, and account filters are applied. Only contacts that match your ICP and program rules move forward.

Verify · V(lead)

Human tele-verification

Our Pune delivery team calls to confirm identity, interest, and, where required, BANT plus your custom qualifying questions.

Output · Q(spec) → L

QA gate & delivery

Dedupe, suppression, and spec validation run on every batch. Out-of-spec records are replaced. Clean leads ship in your format.
