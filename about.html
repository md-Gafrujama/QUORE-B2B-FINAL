About Quore B2B

A delivery operation, not another agency.

We built Quore B2B to do one thing well: fulfill the leads that demand generation teams have already committed to their clients - compliantly, verified, and on spec - so our partners can scale delivery without scaling their own operation.

What we're built on

Nine years of B2B data, an owned audience, and a delivery team.

Our foundation is a proprietary US B2B contact database, developed and refined over roughly nine years and activated under CAN-SPAM. It's ours - built and owned in-house, not licensed or resold - which is what lets our partners deliver from it with confidence.

Delivery runs through our Pune-based tele-verification and SDR team, who confirm every lead by phone before it ships. That combination - proprietary data plus human verification - is what lets our partners resell our fulfillment under their own name with confidence.
