BANT / tele-verified

Sales-ready, confirmed by a human

Our Pune delivery team calls every BANT lead to confirm Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline - and captures the prospect's current solution and the challenge they're trying to solve. Leads that don't clear the criteria never reach you. What you hand your client is ready for a sales conversation.

  • B

    Budget

    Spending capacity in range for the offer, confirmed on the call.

  • A

    Authority

    Decision-making role or clear influence in the buying process.

  • N

    Need

    A defined pain your client's solution addresses.

  • T

    Timeline

    A stated window to evaluate or buy.
